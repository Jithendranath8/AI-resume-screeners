# Unused hosted attempts

Two hosted attempts at the real-PDF experiment were started before the recorded run (`../pdf_llama32_3b_01/`) and were not used: Gemini returned no responses, and Groq stopped at 140 of 210 on a daily token limit.
Only their frozen protocol files are kept here (`pdf_gemini_01_protocol.json`, `pdf_groq_01_protocol.json`), to show that the hypothesis and both thresholds were identical to those of the recorded run.
The incomplete responses were removed from the repository; they remain in git history at commit 6ae899d.
