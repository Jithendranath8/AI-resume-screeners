What happens behind the demo?

  1. Normalize: remove zero-width separators and fold a limited set of look-alike characters.
  2. Represent: convert the text into character TF-IDF features learned from the training split.
  3. Predict: apply logistic regression and the recorded validation-selected threshold of 0.5.
  4. Compare: show the keyword baseline and the trained detector on the same input.

The model is reconstructed from the frozen training split when the demo starts. It does not learn from pasted text. Scores show model output, not a calibrated probability that someone is malicious. We do not claim to identify which words caused a model decision.

Research result and limits

On the recorded test set, the trained detector achieved 93.75% recall and 18.75% false positives. The hypothesis required no more than 10% false positives, so it was not supported. There were 32 source test sentences, each with three encodings. These demo presets include known research examples, not new evaluation evidence.

Longer résumé text and line-by-line processing have not been validated as a document-level detector. A flag is a review cue, not a hiring decision; no flag is not proof of safety. No PDF extraction or live LLM screening takes place.
